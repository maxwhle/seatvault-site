Questions, answered.

How is the profit split?

Every arrangement is different. We set terms that fit your seats and how often you release them, and walk through it together on your first call.

Will I need to keep an eye on my listings?

No. We monitor every listing, adjust pricing as game day gets closer, and see it through to the sale. Since every account stays in your name, you can always look in if you want to.

Do I have to release every game?

No. You decide which games to release and how many seats. Keep the games you want, any time.

Where are my tickets sold?

Through your ticketing platform or other secondary sites, depending on what gets you the best result. Every account stays in your name.

How does the money work?

Every sale pays out directly to your account, in your name. At the end of each month, we send you one invoice for our share.

What if I find out last minute that I can’t go?

Send us a text. We’ve sold seats as late as the day of the game.

How do I get started?

Book a 15-minute call or send a text. We’ll learn about your seats and take it from there.
